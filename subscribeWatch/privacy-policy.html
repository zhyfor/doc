SubscribeWatch 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-08-17

中文

SubscribeWatch 是一个浏览器扩展，帮助你跟踪订阅服务、收取扣款提醒，并监控订阅涨价。我们遵循最小化收集原则。

1. 我们收集什么数据

仅收集你主动提供或授权识别的订阅信息：服务名称、金额、币种、计费周期、扣款日期、备注，以及设置项和本地扫描日志。

2. 数据存储在哪里

所有数据只存储在你自己的浏览器本地（chrome.storage.local）。我们不运营任何服务器，不将任何数据上传、同步或分享给任何第三方。数据不会离开你的设备。

3. 邮箱扫描与网页捕获如何工作

  • 邮箱扫描：仅在你在 Gmail / Outlook 页面点击「扫描收据」按钮时，读取当前可见的邮件发件人与主题，并在你点开某封邮件后读取其正文，用于识别订阅信息。不使用 OAuth API，不存储邮件全文。
  • 网页捕获：当你访问包含订阅/Billing 关键词与金额模式的页面时，读取页面文本以提取订阅信息。其他页面不采集。

4. 我们不收集什么

我们不收集：个人身份信息、地理位置、浏览历史、密码、银行卡/支付凭证，也不连接任何银行账户。

5. 数据导出与删除

扩展的「设置」页提供一键导出（JSON）与一键清空全部数据。卸载扩展即永久删除本地数据。

6. 变更

若本政策发生变更，我们会在扩展更新说明中告知。

7. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请联系：[fenghoer@163.com]

English

SubscribeWatch is a browser extension that helps you track subscriptions, receive billing reminders, and monitor price increases. We follow a minimal-data philosophy.

1. What we collect

Only subscription information you provide or authorize us to identify: service name, amount, currency, billing cycle, billing date, notes, plus settings and local scan logs.

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored locally in your browser only (chrome.storage.local). We operate no servers and never upload, sync, or share any data with any third party. Data never leaves your device.

3. How email scanning & web capture work

  • Email scanning: Only when you click "Scan receipts" on Gmail/Outlook pages, we read the visible email sender/subject, and the body of an email you open, to extract subscription info. No OAuth API is used and full email text is never stored.
  • Web capture: When you visit a page containing subscription/billing keywords and amount patterns, we read page text to extract subscription info. No other pages are collected.

4. What we do NOT collect

No personal identity, geolocation, browsing history, passwords, or payment credentials. We never connect to bank accounts.

5. Export & deletion

The Options page provides one-click JSON export and one-click full wipe. Uninstalling the extension permanently deletes local data.

6. Changes

Any policy changes will be noted in extension release notes.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: [fenghoer@163.com]
